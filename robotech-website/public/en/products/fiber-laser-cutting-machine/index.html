Overview

The Robotech fiber laser cutting machine is the first choice for Saudi fabrication shops, steel-structure factories, stainless kitchen makers and metal signage producers. It cuts carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper and galvanised sheet at high speed with clean edges that usually need no secondary finishing.

It is built on a welded, stress-relieved bed for long-term stability, with laser sources from global brands such as MAX Photonics, high-response servo drives and nesting software that reduces scrap and increases the profit of every sheet.

FAQ

FAQ: Fiber Laser Cutting Machine for Metal Sheets

What is the maximum thickness a fiber laser can cut?

It depends on laser power and metal. As a rough guide, a 3 kW machine cuts carbon steel up to about 20 mm and stainless up to about 10 mm, while 6 kW and above suit thicker plate and heavy production. We'll size the power to your actual materials and thicknesses.

Open-type or enclosed machine — what's the difference?

Open-type machines are economical and ideal for workshops. Enclosed machines with an exchange table offer more operator protection and higher output because loading happens during cutting. See the enclosed machine.

Does the machine need gases?

Yes. Oxygen is used for carbon steel, nitrogen for stainless and aluminium to get bright edges, and compressed air can be used on thin sheet to cut costs.
